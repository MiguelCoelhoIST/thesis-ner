Experiment 1 — Simple legal role classifier

Goal:
Classify PER entities into legal roles using sentence context.

Labels:
ARGUIDO, TESTEMUNHA, RELATOR, REU, UNKNOWN

Approach:
CountVectorizer with n-grams + LogisticRegression.

Result:
The classifier correctly predicts simple examples and UNKNOWN prevents forced role assignment.

Conclusion:
The two-step approach is feasible:
Eduardo NER -> PER -> role classifier
