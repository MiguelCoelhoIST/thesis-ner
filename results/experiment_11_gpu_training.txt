Observations:
GPU training reduced runtime from approximately 49 minutes on local CPU to approximately 1.5 minutes on INESC GPU.

The evaluation metrics are similar to Experiment 10, which is expected because the model, dataset and training configuration are the same. The main contribution of this experiment is infrastructure validation and reproducibility on GPU.

The manual evaluation is strong: the model correctly handles multi-entity role-binding examples and also recovers the indirect "testemunho de" case, although with lower confidence (0.54).

Conclusion:
The GPU setup is working correctly and enables much faster experimentation. This makes it feasible to test multiple encoders, datasets, and hyperparameter configurations.
