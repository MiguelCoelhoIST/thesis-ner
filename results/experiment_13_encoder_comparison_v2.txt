Experiment 13 - Encoder comparison on realistic_v2 benchmark

Goal:
Compare multiple sentence embedding encoders for legal role classification using the larger realistic_v2 evaluation benchmark.

Training dataset:
data/processed/roles_v4.jsonl

Evaluation dataset:
data/eval/roles_eval_realistic_v2.jsonl

Evaluation benchmark:
- 240 examples
- generated using GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek
- includes easy, contextual and multi-entity examples
- validated with JSONL validation script
- approximately balanced across labels

Models compared:
1. sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2
2. intfloat/multilingual-e5-base
3. BAAI/bge-m3

Classifier:
LogisticRegression over frozen sentence embeddings

Results:
1. BAAI/bge-m3
   accuracy: 0.5417
   macro_f1: 0.5210
   runtime: 9.44s

2. intfloat/multilingual-e5-base
   accuracy: 0.4917
   macro_f1: 0.4596
   runtime: 7.03s

3. sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2
   accuracy: 0.4042
   macro_f1: 0.3755
   runtime: 7.51s

Observation:
The model ranking changed significantly compared with the previous 17-example evaluation set. In the smaller benchmark, paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2 appeared to be the best model. However, on the larger and harder realistic_v2 benchmark, BAAI/bge-m3 achieved the best macro F1.

Interpretation:
This suggests that the original evaluation set was too small and unstable for reliable model comparison. The larger benchmark provides a more robust estimate of model behaviour, especially in contextual and multi-entity cases.

Conclusion:
BAAI/bge-m3 is currently the strongest encoder among the tested models for this legal role classification setup. Future experiments should use roles_eval_realistic_v2.jsonl as the primary evaluation benchmark.
